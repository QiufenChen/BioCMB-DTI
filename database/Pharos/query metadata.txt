  UniProt - The UniProt ID for this protein
  Symbol - The approved gene symbol
  Name - The name of the protein
  Target Development Level - Development levels of targets in the list. The categories are based on the degree to which the targets are studied, as evidenced by publications, tool compounds, and other features.
  IDG Family - Target family designations generated by IDG-KMC groups at UNM and UMiami.
  Novelty - Tin-X metric for the relative scarcity of specific publications for this target.

SQL Query:
SELECT
  protein.id AS `id`,
  protein.uniprot AS `UniProt`,
  protein.sym AS `Symbol`,
  target.name AS `Name`,
  target.tdl AS `Target Development Level`,
  CASE
    target.fam
    WHEN 'IC' THEN 'Ion Channel'
    WHEN 'TF; Epigenetic' THEN 'TF-Epigenetic'
    WHEN 'TF' THEN 'Transcription Factor'
    WHEN 'NR' THEN 'Nuclear Receptor'
    ELSE IF(target.fam IS NULL, 'Other', target.fam)
  END AS `IDG Family`,
  tinx_novelty.score AS `Novelty`
FROM
  `protein`
  LEFT JOIN `t2tc` ON `protein`.`id` = `t2tc`.`protein_id`
  LEFT JOIN `target` AS `target` ON `t2tc`.`target_id` = `target`.`id`
  LEFT JOIN `tinx_novelty` AS `tinx_novelty` ON `protein`.`id` = `tinx_novelty`.`protein_id`
ORDER BY
  `id` ASC
LIMIT
  250000
